{{ siteConfig.name }} is a contemporary take on Lippan mirror art — the traditional craft where clay and mirror are worked together so that light scatters across a room long after the sun goes down.

Every piece is handcrafted with moulding clay and delicate glass mirrors, patterned freehand and finished by hand. Because nothing is moulded or machine-made, no two pieces are ever identical — each one comes alive differently with the light in your home.

We work to order as often as we work from the collection, so if you have a size, colour or motif in mind, tell us on WhatsApp and we'll make it for you.
